# 🎬 Netflix Content Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes Netflix's content catalog to understand the platform's content strategy, distribution of movies and TV shows, release trends, genres, countries, ratings, and content duration.

The analysis was performed using **Microsoft Excel** to clean, transform, analyze, and visualize the dataset through an interactive dashboard.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze the overall distribution of **Movies vs TV Shows**
* Identify the **most popular genres**
* Analyze Netflix's **content growth over the years**
* Understand content distribution across **countries**
* Examine **content ratings** and audience categories
* Analyze movie durations and TV show seasons
* Identify trends that can help understand Netflix's content strategy

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 1. Movies dominate Netflix's catalog

Movies make up a significantly larger portion of Netflix's content compared with TV Shows, indicating a strong focus on movie-based content.

### 2. Netflix's content library expanded significantly

The number of titles released increased substantially over the years, showing Netflix's rapid expansion of its content library.

### 3. Drama and International content are major categories

Drama and international genres represent important portions of the Netflix catalog, highlighting the platform's focus on diverse and globally appealing content.

### 4. Netflix has a strong international presence

The catalog contains content from multiple countries, demonstrating Netflix's strategy of serving audiences across different regions and cultures.

### 5. Mature audience content is prominent

Ratings such as **TV-MA and TV-14** represent a major share of the catalog, indicating a strong presence of content targeted toward mature and teenage audiences.

### 6. Movie durations are concentrated around feature-length formats

Most movies fall within a typical feature-film duration range, while a smaller number of titles have significantly shorter or longer runtimes.

## 💡 Business Takeaways

The analysis suggests that Netflix's content strategy emphasizes **large-scale content production, international reach, diverse genres, and mature-audience programming**.

Understanding these patterns can help stakeholders identify content trends, audience preferences, and opportunities for future content acquisition or production.
